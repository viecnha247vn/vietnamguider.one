# Hanoi to Ninh Binh: the honest guide

Ninh Binh — think Tam Coc, Trang An and Hang Mua — is only about 95 km south of
Hanoi, so it's an easy day trip or overnight. The journey takes roughly two
hours. Here's how the options compare.

## Which should you pick?

If you're counting every dong, the **train** is a genuine pleasure and barely
costs anything. Most travellers prefer the **limousine van** for the hotel
pickup. If you want to see Trang An, Hang Mua and Bai Dinh in one day without
juggling taxis, hiring a **private car** for the day pays for itself.

## Day trip or overnight?

You can see the highlights in a long day trip, but staying one night lets you
catch Hang Mua at sunrise before the tour buses arrive — the single best move
in Ninh Binh.

## Before you go

Bring cash for boat rides and entrance tickets, and an **eSIM** so you can
navigate the back roads between sights. Pick your option above and go.
