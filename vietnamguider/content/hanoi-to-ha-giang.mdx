# Hanoi to Ha Giang: the honest guide

Ha Giang is the gateway to the famous Ha Giang Loop, about 300 km north of
Hanoi. There is no train, so you'll travel by road — expect roughly six to
seven hours. Here's how to choose.

## Which should you pick?

Most people take the **overnight sleeper bus**: you leave Hanoi in the evening
and wake up in Ha Giang ready to ride, saving a night's accommodation. Take the
limousine van if you'd rather travel by day in a bit more comfort, or a private
car if you're a group.

## A note on the loop

The Ha Giang Loop is a 3–4 day motorbike route. If you don't ride, book an
"easy rider" — a local driver who takes you on the back of their bike. Sort your
Ha Giang arrival first (above), then arrange the loop once you're in town or
through your hostel.

## Before you go

Nights get cold in the mountains — pack layers and a light rain jacket. Buy an
**eSIM** in advance; signal on the loop is patchy but you'll want data in town.
Pick your ride above and start planning the loop.
