roughly four to five hours away. It's an easy weekend trip. Here's how the

## What about the train?

A train runs from Ho Chi Minh City to Phan Thiet (the town next to Mui Ne),
followed by a short taxi. It's a pleasant ride if the timings suit you, though
the limousine van is usually simpler door to door.

## Which should you pick?

The **limousine van** wins for most travellers: cheap, comfy and straight to
your hotel. Take the sleeper bus to save a few dollars, or a private car if
you're a group.

## Before you go

Mui Ne is Vietnam's kite-surfing capital — the wind is strongest roughly
November to April. Grab an **eSIM** for the trip and book the sunrise dune tour
your first evening. Pick your ride above and go.
