Chinese border. The road is now a fast expressway most of the way, so the drive
takes roughly five to six hours. Here is every sensible option, ranked by what

## What about the train?

There is a scenic overnight train from Hanoi to **Lao Cai**, followed by a
one-hour transfer up to Sa Pa. It's a lovely experience and worth it if you
love rail travel, but it is slower door-to-door than the bus and costs more
once you add the transfer. For pure convenience, the limousine van wins.

## Which should you pick?

Backpackers on a budget take the sleeper bus. Most independent travellers are
happiest in the **limousine van** — a few dollars more for hotel pickup and a
comfier seat. Choose a private car if you're a small group or want to stop for
photos on the way up.

## Before you go

Sa Pa is cool year-round and cold in winter — pack a warm layer. Grab an
**eSIM** before you travel so you have data on arrival, and book buses a day
ahead in peak season (roughly September to November). Pick your tier above and
you're set.
