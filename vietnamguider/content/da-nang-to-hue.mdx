# Da Nang to Hue: the honest guide

Hue, Vietnam's old imperial capital, is about 100 km north of Da Nang. The
journey crosses the spectacular Hai Van Pass, so how you travel is part of the
experience — usually two to three hours. Here's how to choose.

## Which should you pick?

This is one route where the **journey is the attraction**. The train is
gorgeous and almost free; the **private car over the Hai Van Pass** turns the
transfer into a mini-tour with stops. Take the limousine van only if you just
want to get there quickly and cheaply.

## Before you go

Give yourself a full day for the Imperial City and the royal tombs. Grab an
**eSIM** for the trip, and if you love a scenic ride, take the train one way and
the pass the other. Pick your option above and go.
