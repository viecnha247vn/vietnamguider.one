## Which should you pick?

For Phu Quoc, **just fly**. Flights are short, frequent, and often cost about
the same as the all-day bus-and-ferry once you value your time. Only take the
ferry combo if you specifically want the overland route or are travelling on the
tightest budget. Add a private transfer if your resort is far from the airport.

## Before you go

Phu Quoc is warmest and driest roughly November to April. Grab an **eSIM**
before you fly, and book resorts ahead in peak season. Pick your option above
and go.
