# Da Nang to Hoi An: the honest guide

Hoi An sits about 30 km south of Da Nang, so this is a short, easy transfer —
usually 40 minutes to an hour. Most travellers fly into Da Nang and head
straight to Hoi An. Here's how the options compare.

## Which should you pick?

For such a short hop the **private car** is only a few dollars more than a Grab
and lets you stop at the Marble Mountains — a nice bonus on the way in. If
you're counting every dong, the local bus works; just have small notes ready.

## Airport transfers

Coming straight from Da Nang airport? The same options apply, and a pre-booked
private car waiting with your name is the least stressful after a flight.

## Before you go

Hoi An's Ancient Town is best at dusk when the lanterns come on. Grab an
**eSIM** at the airport so you can book a ride the moment you land, then pick
your transfer above.
