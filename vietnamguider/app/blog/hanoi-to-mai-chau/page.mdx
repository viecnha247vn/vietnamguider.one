# Hanoi to Mai Chau: the honest guide

Mai Chau is a peaceful valley of stilt-house homestays and rice fields about
140 km southwest of Hanoi. It's a popular weekend escape, roughly three to four
hours away by road. Here's how to get there.

## Which should you pick?

The **limousine van** is the clear winner here: it's cheap, and unlike the
public bus it takes you all the way into the valley rather than dropping you on
the highway. Choose the bus only to save a few dollars, or a private car if
you're travelling as a group.

## Why go to Mai Chau

It's the closest place to Hanoi where you can cycle between Thai and Muong
villages, sleep in a stilt house, and see rice terraces without the long haul to
Sa Pa. One or two nights is plenty.
