## Which should you pick?

If you are on a tight budget and don't mind a slower ride, the public bus is
hard to beat. Most independent travellers are happiest with the **limousine
van**: it is only a few dollars more, picks you up from your hotel, and skips
the trip out to the bus station. Take a private car if you are three or more
people or want to stop for photos along the way.
