# Hanoi to Cat Ba Island: the honest guide

Cat Ba is the big island beside Lan Ha Bay, about 150 km east of Hanoi. Because
it's an island, every route involves a water crossing, so the options are
package deals rather than a single ride. Expect roughly three to four hours
door to door.

## Which should you pick?

The **combo ticket** is the no-brainer for solo travellers and couples — one
booking, all legs included. Pay a little more for the **limousine package** if
you want hotel pickup and fewer changes. A private transfer only makes sense for
groups or families with luggage.

## Cat Ba vs a Ha Long cruise

If your main goal is cruising the bay, note that boats from Cat Ba explore **Lan
Ha Bay** — quieter and, many say, prettier than the main Ha Long tourist route.
Staying on the island also lets you hike Cat Ba National Park and kayak at your
own pace.
